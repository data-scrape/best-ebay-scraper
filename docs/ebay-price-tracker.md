# eBay Price Tracker

Track public eBay product prices over time.

## Supported Data

- Current Price
- Original Price
- Shipping Cost
- Availability
- Seller
- Product URL

## Use Cases

- Price Monitoring
- Competitive Pricing
- Deal Tracking
- Product Research
